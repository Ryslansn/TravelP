ATLAS Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026.

ATLAS is a personal map of the places you have been. Everything you add is private by default — your profile, your places and your photos are visible only to you until you choose to publish them.

1. Who processes your data

The data controller is JenkinsLab, Uzhhorod, Ukraine. For any privacy question, contact betench2@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account

  • your email address, used solely to sign you in with a one-time link or code;
  • an internal user identifier and its mapping to your authentication record.

Profile

  • username, display name, bio;
  • an avatar, if you add one;
  • visibility settings and your chosen map style.

Your places

  • coordinates you place by hand on the map;
  • date and time of the visit, if you provide them;
  • title, place types, country and city;
  • your note;
  • photos you attach.

Technical

  • error and crash records containing no personal data.

3. What we do not collect

This is the current state of the app rather than a promise, and it is verifiable: the shipped build declares no location permission at all.

  • Device location. The app never requests or reads it. A place's coordinates come only from where you tapped on the map;
  • background location or route tracking — neither exists;
  • advertising identifiers, contacts, calendar, microphone;
  • payment data — the app is free and has no purchases;
  • biometrics or identity documents.

4. Photos

A photo is processed on your device before it is sent: it is compressed, and its EXIF metadata — including capture coordinates, camera model and timestamp — is stripped. Only the cleaned image reaches the server.

Files are held in private storage. Links to them are short-lived and signed, and the database stores an object key and a checksum rather than a permanent address. Photos are deleted along with their place or your account.

6. Who receives data

We do not sell data and do not share it with advertising networks. The processors the service depends on:

  • Supabase — database, file storage and authentication;
  • MapTiler — map tiles. It receives requests for the map fragments you view, but not your places, photos or notes;
  • Apple and Google — app distribution.

7. Retention

Data lives as long as your account does. Once you request deletion, your profile and share links become unavailable immediately, and records and files are removed by a deletion job. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

8. Your rights

  • obtain a copy of your data — the app offers an export request;
  • correct your data — by editing your profile and places;
  • delete your account — how to do it;
  • withdraw consent to publication — by turning off the public profile;
  • lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

9. Security

Row-level access control means one user cannot read another's records even outside the app. Public data is served through a separate view listing exactly which fields are exposed. Privileged keys are used only on the server and never ship inside the app. Coordinates, email addresses and note contents are never written to logs or sent to analytics.

10. Children

The service is not directed to people under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

We will announce material changes in the app. The revision date is always at the top of this page.
