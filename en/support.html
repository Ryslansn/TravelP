ATLAS Support

Last updated 31 August 2026.

Write to betench2@gmail.com. We reply within a few working days.

What to include

  • your device model and OS version;
  • the app version — it is at the bottom of the settings screen;
  • what you were doing when the problem appeared.

Never send passwords or sign-in codes. We do not need them and they cannot help.

Common questions

The sign-in email does not arrive

Check your spam folder. If the app says "too many attempts", that is a limit on how many emails the mail service will send, not a problem with your account — try again in an hour.

The map looks coarse

The "Planet" style deliberately shows country outlines rather than streets — it is meant for the overall view. Switch styles in the picker to place pins precisely.

How do I delete my account

Settings → Delete account. Details are on a separate page.

Who can see my places

Nobody, until you make your profile public. Both your profile and every place are private by default.
