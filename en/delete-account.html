Deleting your ATLAS account

Last updated 31 August 2026.

You can delete your account yourself from inside the app. This is not a sign-out — the data is actually removed.

In the app

  1. open Settings;
  2. choose Delete account;
  3. confirm.

If you cannot reach the app

Email [FILL IN: email] from the address the account is registered to. We will match the address and complete the deletion within 30 days. We ask for no other proof of identity and accept no documents.

What is deleted

Immediately, at the moment of the request:

  • all your places, with their coordinates, dates, notes and types;
  • the records of your photos;
  • your profile — username, bio, settings;
  • any export requests.

At the same moment your profile stops being public and every share link you created is deactivated — they stop opening straight away.

What follows shortly after

The photo files themselves are removed from storage by a background job. The links to them are deleted immediately, so the images are unreachable from the moment you make the request.

The account stays in a "deletion pending" state until that job finishes. Database backups are overwritten within [FILL IN: period, typically 30 days].

What we must keep

A record that the deletion happened — when it was requested and when it completed. It exists to prove the request was honoured and contains none of your content.

Can it be undone

No. Deletion is final. If you want to keep your places, use the data export in settings first.
