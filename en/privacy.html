Trevia Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026.

Trevia is a personal map of the places you have been. Your places are private by default: nobody sees them until you open your profile or add a friend.

1. Who processes your data

The data controller is JenkinsLab, Uzhhorod, Ukraine. For any privacy question, contact betench2@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account

  • your email address, used solely to sign you in with a one-time code;
  • an internal user identifier and its mapping to your authentication record.

Profile

  • username, display name, bio;
  • an avatar, if you add one;
  • visibility settings and your chosen map style.

Your places

  • coordinates you place by hand on the map;
  • date and time of the visit, if you provide them;
  • title, place types, country and city;
  • your note;
  • photos you attach.

Friends

  • who you sent a friend request to, and who sent one to you;
  • the state of the connection — pending, accepted, declined — and when it changed.

In-app currency and purchases

  • your in-app currency balance and its movements;
  • the fact of a purchase and how long it lasts.

Advertising — not collected yet

The current version carries no advertising, so no advertising identifier is collected. When advertising arrives, the list above will gain your device's advertising identifier and the fact that an ad was watched with the reward granted for it — and we will say so in the app before it does.

Technical

  • error and crash records containing no personal data.

3. What we do not collect

  • Device location. The app never requests or reads it — the shipped build declares no location permission at all. A place's coordinates come only from where you tapped on the map;
  • background location or route tracking — neither exists;
  • contacts, calendar, microphone;
  • payment details. Card numbers and billing data are handled by Apple and Google. We never see or store them — all that reaches us is the fact that a purchase happened;
  • biometrics or identity documents.

4. Photos

A photo is processed on your device before it is sent: it is compressed, and its EXIF metadata — including capture coordinates, camera model and timestamp — is stripped. Only the cleaned image reaches the server.

Files are held in private storage. Links to them are short-lived and signed, and the database stores an object key and a checksum rather than a permanent address. Photos are deleted along with their place or your account.

5. Advertising

The current version carries no advertising. No advertising identifier is collected, and no ad network receives anything from the app. This section describes how it will work once advertising arrives — and we will say so in the app before it does.

It will be rewarded video: you decide whether to watch one in exchange for in-app currency. Ads will never be shown without your action and never interrupt what you are doing.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To select an ad and to prevent fraud, Google receives your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and your interaction with the ad. Google processes these as an independent controller under its own policy.

The ad network never receives your places, coordinates, photos or notes. None of them take any part in selecting an ad.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, personalised ads are shown only with your consent, which we ask for separately and which you can change at any time in settings. The advertising identifier itself can be reset or switched off in your device settings, under privacy and ads.

6. Purchases and in-app currency

Payments are processed by the App Store and Google Play. We receive their confirmation and store only what follows from it: which level of access you hold and until when.

In-app currency is a balance inside your account. It is not money, holds no value outside the app, cannot be exchanged back into money and cannot be transferred to another user. We keep its balance and its movements so that the same ad view cannot be rewarded twice.

7. Friends

A friendship is confirmed by both sides: your request reveals nothing until the other person accepts it.

Once accepted, a friend sees your profile and the places you marked as visible to friends — including their coordinates and photos. Places that stayed private are seen by nobody but you.

You can end the connection at any time, and access stops immediately. What someone has already seen or saved to their own device cannot be recalled — bear that in mind when opening up places connected to where you live.

9. Who receives data

We do not sell your data. The processors and recipients the service depends on:

  • Supabase — database, file storage and authentication;
  • Google AdMob — rewarded advertising; receives the advertising identifier and device data, but not your content;
  • MapTiler — map tiles. It receives requests for the map fragments you view, but not your places, photos or notes;
  • Apple and Google — app distribution and payment processing;
  • other users — only those you added as friends or opened a public profile to.

10. Retention

Data lives as long as your account does. Once you request deletion, your profile and share links become unavailable immediately, and records and files are removed by a deletion job. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Purchase records are kept longer where tax or accounting law requires it.

11. Your rights

  • obtain a copy of your data — the app offers an export request;
  • correct your data — by editing your profile and places;
  • delete your account — how to do it;
  • withdraw consent to publication — by turning off the public profile or removing a friend;
  • withdraw consent to personalised advertising — in settings;
  • lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

12. Security

Row-level access control means one user cannot read another's records even outside the app. Public data is served through a separate view listing exactly which fields are exposed. Privileged keys are used only on the server and never ship inside the app. Coordinates, email addresses and note contents are never written to logs or sent to analytics.

Ad rewards are granted by the server on confirmation from the ad network, not by the app itself — which is why a balance cannot be forged from a device.

13. Children

The service is not directed to people under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data and do not show them advertising.

14. Changes

We will announce material changes in the app. The revision date is always at the top of this page.
